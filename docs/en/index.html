Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account and my data?

Settings → Account → Delete my account. It is immediate and permanent: your account and your cloud save are erased from our servers, and everything the app keeps on the phone is erased. Without an online account, only the phone is affected.

Where is my data?

On your phone. It only goes online if you turn on "Save my progress" with Apple or Google. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does the camera send images anywhere?

No. Scoring happens on the device, frame by frame, with a model embedded in the app. Nothing is stored or sent.

I changed phones, where is my progress?

If you had turned on cloud save with Apple or Google, sign in again from Settings → Account: it comes back. Otherwise, it stayed on the old phone, and the new one starts from scratch.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Game → Language: French, English, or the phone's language.

The counter got it wrong, what do I do?

You can undo the last dart and enter it again; with the camera, a correction sheet lets you fix the visit. Good lighting and a board well centred in the frame avoid most errors.
