What's inside

Everything to improve,
without losing your evening.

Small units to chain after work, and real games when friends drop by.

A path: 12 chapters, 100 units

Each chapter is a room of the club. Units unlock in order, a boss waits at the end, and you go from hitting the 20 to the finishes that matter.

Three-minute drills

Hit the 20 and the 19, score exactly 41, land four in a row… Eight drills per unit, three stars to earn.

The camera scores for you

Set the phone facing the board: it sees every dart and keeps score on its own. Everything runs on the device, no image ever leaves it.

A referee who calls it

Every visit is called out loud, like on TV. One button to mute it when the neighbours are asleep.

One hundred and eighty!

Solo, with friends or against a bot, with the rules nobody ever bothers to re-read. And a game in progress picks up right where you left it.

Bots that don't go easy

Five levels, from Sunday player to pro. They throw at real speed, or instantly when you're in a hurry. And a real bull-off to decide who starts.

A flame to keep alive

One unit a day is enough. A reminder at the time you choose, and the streak goes on.

Three stars, a recap, the next unit

At the end of each unit: first-dart accuracy, darts thrown, time taken. And the next one unlocks.

How it works

Three minutes to get started.

Then a little every day: that's the whole trick.

1

Download Dartumo

Free on iPhone and iPad. A nickname, how many minutes a day, and that's it.

2

Set the phone facing the board

The camera scores your darts. Not in the mood? The keypad is right there, and so is the tap-the-board input.

3

Start your first unit

Eight drills, three stars, a flame that lights up. See you tomorrow.

The crew

You never play
alone.

A mascot, six companions and a villain. All drawn inside the app, stroke by stroke.

The mascot

Umo

Umo tells you what to aim at, jumps for joy at every hit and sulks (a little) at every miss.

One per chapter

The companions

In the colour of their room, they hang around the track, cheer you on at the bend and doze off in the chapters still locked.

The end-of-chapter boss

The Bot

He smirks, he focuses, he loses his temper. And he's a very sore loser. Up to you how far up the levels you follow him.
