In short

  • DuoDarts is free, with no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • On first launch, the app creates an anonymous guest account: a technical identifier, with no email and no password.
  • Your games, stats and progress stay on your phone. They only go online if you turn on cloud save with Apple or Google.
  • The camera counts your darts on the device: no image is ever stored or sent.
  • You can erase everything yourself, at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my account.

2. What data we process, and why

The app processes very little data, and most of it never leaves your phone. Here is everything, category by category.

2.1 The guest account, created automatically

On first launch with a network connection, our host Supabase assigns the app a random technical identifier (a UUID) and a session token, kept on the phone. No email, no password, no name: this identifier says nothing about you. Without a network, the app works offline, with no account.

Why: to run the app, attach your profile, allow cloud save if you turn it on, and delete your account on request. Legal basis: performance of the contract (the terms of use).

2.2 Your first name or nickname

The app asks for a first name on first launch; you may use a nickname, and change it in Settings. It is kept on the phone and, as soon as the app has an account, stored in your online profile so it can be shown back to you.

Why: to call you by your name and find your profile again. Legal basis: performance of the contract.

2.3 Your games, stats and progress

Everything you do in the app is recorded on your phone, in the app's private storage: your progress on the path (units, stars, XP), your day streak, your games (X01 and the other modes: scores, visits, checkouts, durations), the stats you choose to record, and your settings (language, sounds, vibrations, mode settings, training pace, reminder time, camera zoom).

This data does not leave the phone unless you turn on cloud save (2.5). Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may hold a copy, depending on your system settings.

Why: to run the app. Legal basis: performance of the contract.

2.4 Regulars: the people you play with

In Play, you can save "regulars": a name or first name, then their scores and game stats. This information is on your phone, and only there (unless cloud save is on, 2.5). Nobody else sees it. You can rename or delete a regular at any time, in Stats or in Settings.

You enter these names yourself: do so with the agreement of the people concerned.

Why: to keep score and stats among friends. Legal basis: our legitimate interest, and yours, in running a multiplayer game.

2.5 Cloud save, with Apple or Google (optional)

When this option is offered and you turn it on, the app signs you in with your Apple or Google account. We then receive that account's identifier, the email address Apple or Google passes on to us (Apple may provide a relay address that hides yours) and, if provided, your first name. Your guest account becomes a permanent account.

From then on, a copy of the data described in 2.3 and 2.4 is sent to our servers after each change, so you can find your progress on another device. Games in progress are not synced.

Apple and Google process your sign-in on their side under their own policies (Apple, Google). We receive nothing beyond what is listed above.

Why: to find your progress anywhere. Legal basis: your consent, given when you turn the option on. "Sign out" leaves the save on your account for a later sign-in; "Delete my account" erases it.

2.6 The camera (optional)

If you turn on camera scoring, the phone films the board and a detection model embedded in the app spots the darts, frame by frame, on the device. No image or video is stored, or sent to us or to anyone. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Legal basis: your consent, given through the system permission.

2.7 The daily reminder (optional)

If you turn it on, the app schedules a local notification at the time you choose, directly on the phone. There is no notification server and no push token: the reminder time stays on your device.

Legal basis: your consent, given through the system permission.

2.8 Technical data

Like any online service, our host keeps technical logs of the app's requests (IP address, date, type of request) for the security and proper operation of the service. They are kept for a few days.

The app contains no analytics tool, no crash reporting and no advertising identifier.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

2.9 When you write to us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. The app's "Email us" button pre-fills the app version, the system version and your account identifier to help us understand the problem; you can delete them before sending.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad network, no advertising identifier.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data, with anyone.
  • No analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making about you.
  • No location, no access to your contacts, photos or microphone.
  • No cookies in the app.

4. Who has access to your data

Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd, Singapore) hosts the account, the profile and the cloud save as our processor, on Amazon Web Services servers located in Paris (eu-west-3 region). Supabase is bound by a data processing agreement and may only use this data to provide us with hosting.

Apple and Google are only involved if you choose to sign in with them (2.5). The App Store and Google Play, which distribute the app, also collect their own data (downloads, reviews, aggregated crashes) under their policies; we only see anonymous statistics.

Nobody else. We would only disclose your data under a legal obligation, at the request of a competent authority.

Transfers outside the European Union: your data is hosted in France. Supabase, a company based in Singapore, may access it from abroad for operations and support, under the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission.

6. Your rights

You can access your data, rectify it, erase it, ask for its portability, restrict its processing or object to it, and give instructions about what happens to it after your death.

The easiest way is in the app: edit your name, rename or delete a regular, "Restart the path", and "Delete my account", which erases everything, on our servers and on the phone, without asking us anything.

For anything else, write to {{emailLink}}; we reply within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or with the authority of your own country.

7. Children

DuoDarts is suitable for all ages and asks for no email, date of birth or photo. If you are under 15, a parent's agreement is required to turn on cloud save with Apple or Google. A parent who wishes to have a child's data erased can write to us.

8. Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). On the server, each account can only read and write its own data (row-level access rules); the app embeds no secret key. On the phone, data lives in the app's private storage, protected by the system.
