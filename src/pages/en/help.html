Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account and my data?

Settings → Account → Delete my account. It is immediate and permanent: your account and your profile are erased from our servers, and everything the app keeps on the phone is erased. Without an online account, only the phone is affected.

Where is my data?

On your phone: your progress, games, stats and regulars. Online, there is only your nickname, in an anonymous profile (no email, no name) that is also used to delete your account. If you agree, the nicknames the referee has to say also go through ElevenLabs. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does the camera send images anywhere?

No. Scoring happens on the device, frame by frame, with a model embedded in the app. Nothing is stored or sent. The first use needs a network connection: the phone fetches from Apple the key that unlocks the model.

I changed phones, where is my progress?

It stayed on the old phone: for now, progress is only kept on the phone you play on. The new one starts from scratch, and uninstalling the app erases your progress. A backup with Apple or Google is planned for a future version.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Game → Language: French, English, or the phone's language.

The counter got it wrong, what do I do?

You can undo the last dart and enter it again; with the camera, a correction sheet lets you fix the visit. Good lighting and a board well centred in the frame avoid most errors.
